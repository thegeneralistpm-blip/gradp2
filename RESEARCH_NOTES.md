# Blinkit discovery-engine research log

## Initial corpus

The dashboard starts with 8 qualitative public signals collected on 30 July 2026. These signals are a directional discovery exercise, not a representative sample or a quantified estimate of Blinkit customers.

| Source | Signals used | Why it is included |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Apple App Store | 3 | Customer reports on navigation, item quality and availability |
| Reddit | 3 | Category-specific trust and shopping-habit discussion |
| LinkedIn UX audit | 1 | Independent observation of category-navigation overload |
| Indus Appstore | 1 | Positive feedback on assortment, value and delivery |

## Initial findings

1. **Trust must precede discovery for considered categories.** Skincare and premium/general merchandise feedback contains authenticity and quality concerns. Recommendations should include trusted-brand indicators, product ratings, and return/replacement reassurance.
2. **Quick commerce is mentally positioned as a replenishment channel.** One community discussion explicitly contrasts routine groceries with expensive products. Growth prompts should attach a low-risk adjacent need to a familiar re-order mission.
3. **More category choice does not automatically create discovery.** The UX audit describes an overloaded home screen. The product hypothesis is to surface a small, behaviour- and mission-based set of relevant category invitations instead of a broad category grid.
